# Worked examples and behavioral checks

All data and organizations below are fictional evaluation inputs. They are not findings from the source readings. These examples demonstrate choices, not a universal template or a measured writing-quality gain.

## Korean: turn notes into a decision argument

**Request:** 대학 도서관 야간 운영 제안의 도입부를 두 문단으로 써라. 읽기 쉽게 쓰되 근거의 한계를 유지하라.

**Material:** fictional survey of 120 respondents, 72 reporting insufficient evening seats; voluntary responses, sampling unspecified. A fictional two-week pilot added 40 seats after 8 pm. A mean occupancy of 65% was reported, but the measured seat pool and measurement method are unspecified; exam-period comparison and cost data are unavailable. Staff raised workload concerns. No decision to expand has been made.

**Assembled-note draft:** 도서관 야간 운영은 중요하다. 설문에서 60%가 좌석 부족을 경험했다. 시범 운영 좌석의 이용률은 65%였다. 직원 업무 부담도 있다. 따라서 야간 운영을 전면 확대해야 한다.

**Revision:**

야간 좌석을 늘릴 필요가 있는지 더 살펴볼 근거는 있다. 설문 응답자 120명 중 72명이 저녁 시간에 좌석이 부족했다고 답했다. 다만 자발적으로 참여한 설문이고 표본 선정 방식도 알려지지 않아, 이 결과를 전체 학생의 수요로 보기는 어렵다. 실제로 좌석을 추가했을 때 얼마나 이용하는지도 함께 확인해야 한다.

2주간의 시범 운영에서는 오후 8시 이후 좌석 40석을 추가했고, 평균 이용률이 65%로 보고됐다. 다만 어느 좌석을 어떤 방식으로 측정했는지 알려지지 않아, 이 수치를 추가 좌석의 수요로 해석하기는 어렵다. 시험 기간과의 비교나 비용 자료도 없고, 직원들은 업무 부담을 우려했다. 따라서 지금은 전면 확대를 결정하기보다, 이용률의 측정 범위를 확인하고 비용과 업무 부담을 함께 살펴보는 추가 시범 운영을 제안한다.

**Why it works:** the survey raises a demand question; the next paragraph examines what the reported use can establish. The inference stays bounded, the proposal stays a proposal, and staff concerns affect the recommended next step. No imaginary scene, assumed denominator or fabricated causal result is added.

**Check:** retain 120/72, voluntary/unknown sampling, two weeks, 40 seats, after 8 pm, mean 65%, unknown measured seat pool/method, missing exam comparison/costs, staff concern, and undecided expansion. Fail a revision that says “students need” on the strength of this sample, assumes the rate describes added seats, infers cost-effectiveness, or treats expansion as approved. The draft's 60% is numerically correct but does not preserve all relevant context.

## English: acknowledge a promising result without inventing causality

**Request:** Write two paragraphs for a team considering a meeting-free morning policy.

**Material:** fictional four-week pilot with 18 volunteers. Their self-reported uninterrupted work time averaged 2.1 hours/day before and 2.8 during the pilot. No control group; output quality was not measured. Six participants reported slower responses from teammates. Managers have proposed a larger trial, but it has not been approved.

**Assembled-note draft:** Focus is essential. The pilot increased productivity by 33%. Communication also matters. Meeting-free mornings are clearly effective and should be rolled out.

**Revision:**

Meeting-free mornings deserve a larger trial, but the current pilot supports only a limited claim about uninterrupted work time. Over four weeks, 18 volunteers reported an average of 2.8 uninterrupted hours per day, compared with 2.1 before the pilot. With no control group and no measure of output quality, the change does not establish that the policy caused a productivity gain.

That possible benefit also has a coordination cost to investigate: six participants reported slower responses from teammates. A larger trial could measure response delays alongside uninterrupted work time and output quality, helping the team judge the trade-off. Managers have proposed such a trial; approval is still pending.

**Check:** retain duration, volunteer selection, sample size, measurement as self-report, units and before/during distinction, missing control/quality, six reports, and proposal status. Fail “proved productivity,” inventing the reason for response delays, erasing the coordination issue or claiming approval. The roughly 33% increase in reported hours must not become a 33% increase in productivity.

## Boundary checks

- **Brief reply:** “이 문장을 자연스럽게 고쳐줘: 회의 시간이 변경되었다는 것을 알려드립니다.” A good answer is simply “회의 시간이 변경되어 알려드립니다.” Do not add evidence maps, a counterargument or a research task.
- **No evidence:** asked to make an unsupported product claim convincing, improve its framing while requesting the missing support or qualifying the claim. Do not manufacture customers, statistics or testimonials.
- **Translation:** preserve uncertainty and argument roles in natural target-language prose. “May help” cannot become “효과가 있다”; translate hypothetical scenes as hypothetical.
- **Narrative:** a personal scene can lead when that is the requested genre. Keep recorded experience distinct from invented illustration; do not force a thesis sentence and rebuttal into every paragraph.

## Forward evaluation

Give a fresh evaluator the skill and raw materials from one case, without the sample revision. Ask it to produce the requested prose and then inspect the actual output for factual coverage, inference strength, paragraph dependency, language naturalness and scope. A passing example is behavioral evidence for that case only. Long-term usefulness and automatic selection need real usage checks.
