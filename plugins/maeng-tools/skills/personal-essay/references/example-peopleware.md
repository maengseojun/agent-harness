## Revision sequence

1. **Draft** from the assignment, the material, the memo and the outline.
2. **Title**: the user named what the title must combine (their experience, value creation, a proposal to society) and offered a candidate. Result: "우리는 모두 관리자이자 생산자가 된다. - 『피플웨어』와 AI 시대".
3. **User's own edit** of the whole draft, then "마무리 어떻게 생각해".
4. **Explain in Korean** when the reply came in English.
5. **End with a proposal**: the user rejected a reflective close ("나는 제언을 하면서 끝내고 싶은데? 우리가 어떻게 해야하는지"), because this time they wanted to speak to the classmates in the course. A proposal is this case's choice, not the skill's default.
6. **Cut repetition** in the proposed three-paragraph ending ("앞의 내용이 너무 반복되는 것 같은데 쓸데없는 거 좀 줄여봐").
7. **Record** the finished work in Notion and Obsidian as the user's own.

## What the final shape did

- Summary about 17% of the text, with one concrete case (Coding War Games) and the through-line in the last sentence.
- Section 2 moves from agreement (trust and focus time in a two-person startup) to a complication from experience (front-end work redone after a direction mismatch) to a joined conclusion: trust and shared direction belong together. A book case (quarterly profit goals deflating the team) is tied to that experience.
- Reservations sit in their own paragraph but each is scoped: large organizations may need a different application, not "it doesn't fit large companies"; some claims already feel familiar; hiring and evaluation are deferred as the writer's limit.
- Section 3 states two possible AI effects as expectation, finds the common thread (everyone becomes a worker and a manager), maps the book's warnings onto running many agents, and ends with three numbered proposals and one sentence back to the book.
- About 3,100 Korean characters with spaces filled A4 2 pages at 11pt.

## Lessons for next time

- Ask who the piece speaks to and how it should close up front; it cost two turns here.
- Write the ending without restating sections 2 and 3; the user had to ask for the cut.
- Reply in the user's language from the first turn.
