# Source

- **Author:** written new for eta-craft on 2026-10-13 (Claude, for Henry; END-687). The team's research had only one line for it (the gate row "auction price is monotone and bounded": a fuzz test plus golden vectors from a reference model), so the know-how here is new.
- **What it draws on:**
  - The team's gate research (`sol-gates.md`): that gate row, the golden-vector and differential pattern, and the handler suites "for realistic multi-step flows (auctions, fills)" (section 1.3 and the gate table).
  - Public practice for Dutch auctions in general (a price that is a function of the clock; round the payment in the seller's favour; a price limit from the taker). No library or protocol is copied. A fixed-point `exp` library is named only as "a tested library" and was not read or run.
  - Written new: the code block, the golden vectors (computed with exact integer arithmetic in Python), the two price properties and the mistakes list.
- **Run, not just read:** on 2026-10-13, in a scratch project (forge 1.7.1, solc 0.8.37, OpenZeppelin 5.7.0). The two code blocks compile together as written and lint clean with the inline `forge-lint` comments. Tests passed for: the golden vectors, price monotone and bounded, price never below the exact line, a fill paying at least the exact price and less than one unit more, partial fills capped at what is left, the price limit, a not-started and a sold-out revert, a one-unit fill never free, and a handler campaign (sold plus remaining is constant, the token balance equals `remaining`, the ledger equals the ghosts, the average price is at least the floor). Rounding the payment down failed three tests. Rounding the price's fall up passed the monotone test and failed the exact-line test and the golden vectors. The scratch project is not in this repo.
- **Not run:** the exponential and stepped shapes, the flash-style fill and the fee-on-transfer buy token are described, with no test.
- **Why it is here:** a craft skill: know-how with no eta rule (Testing and quality §8, OPS-A4-16). Where the eta auction needs a rule (its price guard, where sells and buys stop, what a fill measures), the skill says "eta rule: load eta-rebalancing".
- **Open:** no eval yet (`evals/writing-an-auction/`). `eta-rebalancing` should name this skill in its hand-off (Testing and quality §8); that file is in eta-rules and was not changed here.
